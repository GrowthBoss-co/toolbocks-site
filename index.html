Built by an agency that lives in it every day

ToolBocks wasn't designed in a boardroom. It was built at Growth Boss, a full-service marketing agency in Oakville, Ontario, because calling 60 prospects used to take a rep an entire day. Every feature exists because our own SDR team needed it: the fairness dashboard that shows reps how connects are distributed, the sales handoff document that stops deals from getting dropped between sales and delivery, the human-in-the-loop blast approval that keeps a person in front of every send.

Compliance-first telephony

Frequently asked questions

What is ToolBocks?

ToolBocks is an all-in-one sales engagement platform: power dialer, multi-channel cadences, lead generation, AI-generated sales documents, and reporting in one system. It was built by Growth Boss to run its own outbound sales motion and is the same production platform the agency uses daily.

Does ToolBocks integrate with GoHighLevel?

Yes, natively. Your pipeline mirrors GoHighLevel in real time, lead research writes back to contact records, and email and SMS sends route through your GHL account alongside Twilio telephony.

Is ToolBocks a real product or a prototype?

It's live production software running a real agency's outbound today: thousands of dials logged, live cadences running, and client-facing audits and proposals generated on it every week. Book a demo and we'll walk you through the actual screens.

Who is ToolBocks for?

Marketing agencies, SDR teams, and outbound sales operations that want dialing, cadences, lead sourcing, AI deliverables, and reporting in one place instead of stitching together five or six tools.
