Real research, not template filler

An audit is only useful if the prospect recognizes their own business in it. ToolBocks pulls specifics: their review count and star rating, their follower counts, the platform their site runs on, the local keywords they're not ranking for. The output is a published landing page in your brand colors with a persuasive opening line, ready to drop into a cadence email or read from on a call.

It's fast enough to be an outbound weapon. In our own use, a full audit takes roughly two minutes from typing the domain to a published page. That means "I actually took a look at your business" stops being a lie SDRs tell and becomes the literal truth, at scale.

One transcript in, three documents out

The same document engine chains across the whole deal lifecycle:

  • Proposal generator: built directly from the discovery-call transcript, so the proposal reflects what the prospect actually said, not a generic template.
  • Program Strategy: a 90/180-day client roadmap generated from the same context, ready for the kickoff call.
  • Sales Handoff: the sales-to-delivery transition document, which explicitly flags anything the AI could not find so nothing silently falls through the cracks between the closer and the delivery team.

That last point matters more than it sounds. The most expensive failure in an agency isn't losing a deal; it's winning one and then fumbling the handoff. ToolBocks treats the handoff as a first-class document with its own gaps-checklist, because we've lived that failure ourselves.

Powered by your business context

Every document draws on a central Business Library: your brand identity, services, case studies, and a free-text business context that acts as the brain behind every AI output. Set it once, and every audit, proposal, and email your team generates sounds like your company instead of a chatbot.
