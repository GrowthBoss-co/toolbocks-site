Two ways to source

Decision-maker search, powered by Amplemarket data, finds the actual owner or buyer with verified contact details, not the info@ inbox. Google Places sourcing pulls local businesses by category and area: name, address, reviews, and the surface-level signals that tell you whether they're a fit before anyone dials.

Both feed the same pipeline, which mirrors your GoHighLevel account in real time. There's no CSV export/import dance; a sourced lead is a dialable, cadence-ready contact immediately.

Research that does itself

Tag a contact for research and ToolBocks investigates the business: website, reviews, online presence, likely pain points, and writes the findings back into the GHL contact record. When a rep opens the call screen or a cadence email gets drafted, the context is already there. Reps stop spending their mornings on browser tabs and start spending them on conversations.

Outreach with a human in the loop

The Email Blast and SMS Blast tools draft personalized messages with AI, then queue them for human approval before anything sends: one message every two minutes, through your own GHL account and numbers. That pacing is deliberate. It protects your domain reputation and keeps your outreach looking like what it is, a person reaching out, rather than a spam run that torches your deliverability for a quarter.

Replies land in a unified Replies inbox, so interest never gets lost in a shared mailbox, and a reply automatically pulls the prospect out of any active cadence.

From sourced to booked, one system

Because sourcing, research, outreach, dialing, and reporting share one platform, you can trace any booked meeting back to the exact list, cadence, and call that produced it. That's the difference between "outbound feels like it's working" and knowing your cost per meeting.
