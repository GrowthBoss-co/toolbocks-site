A call archive you can actually use

The Dialer Reports log makes every call browsable: recording, transcript, outcome, and disposition, all filterable and searchable. In our own agency's last 90 days that's 1,928 calls with a clean dispositions breakdown a manager reads in seconds: what share went to no-answer, what share said not interested, what share booked a meeting. It's a compliance record, a coaching library, and a QA tool in one screen.

Team dashboard and ROI funnel

The Team Dashboard shows who's dialing, connecting, and booking. The ROI view connects activity to outcomes as a funnel: dials to connects to conversations to meetings, so you can see exactly where a campaign leaks and fix that stage instead of just "making more calls." Campaign Reports break the same numbers out per list and per cadence, which is how you learn that one angle books meetings and another just burns numbers.

AI spend, attributed per person

AI features cost real money, and most platforms hide that in a monthly invoice. ToolBocks has a dedicated AI Costs page that breaks usage down by operation and by user: every audit, proposal, summary, and draft with its token counts and dollar cost. An admin can see what the AI layer costs per rep and per deliverable, and price the motion accordingly. If you resell outbound as a service, this page is your margin calculator.

Roles that match a real sales floor

Access is role-based with three tiers: Master, Sales Manager with full operational access, and SDR/Operator with a focused, limited view. Per-user password resets and login locks are built in, and integration credentials are stored encrypted and never redisplayed in full.
